Question 1
L4-03 (Selection) (EQE - 9.5 points)


An international application was filed by a German applicant as a first application in Germany on 3 September 2022. The international application validly enters the regional phase before the EPO at the end of the normal time limit for EP entry.
(a) When is the first renewal fee (in respect of the third year) due at the EPO and up until when could it have been paid without additional fee?

- Renewal fee must be paid in respect of the third year and each subsequent year - R159(1)(g) EPC
- The renewal fee in respect of the third year may not be validly paid more than six months before it falls due - R51(1) EPC
- Renewal fee for the IA application after EP entry in respect of the coming year shall be due on the last day of the month containing the anniversary of the date of filing of the European patent application (IA in this case). - R51(1) EPC
- If renewal fee is due before entering EP phase, it must be paid on entry to EP phase - A86(1) EPC; R51(1) EPC, A2(1).4 RFeesEPC
- In this case: 
- EP entry date: 3 Sep 2022 [A2(xi) PCT] + 31m [A22(3) PCT; R159(1) EPC; R131(4) EPC] = 3 Apr 2025
- Third year renewal fee date: 3 Sep 2022 [A2(xi) PCT] + 2 yr = 3 Sep 2024 + end of month = 30 Sep 2024.  Falls EARLIER than EP entry date. So MUST be paid immediately on EP entry. - R159(1)(g) EPC; A86(1) EPC; R51(1) EPC
- 3 Apr 2025 is the LAST day the fee may be paid without incurring additional fees.
- First renewal fee (in respect of the third year) is due at the EPO on 3 Apr 2025.

(b) Up until when can it be paid with an additional fee?

- It may still be validly paid within 6m of 3 Apr 2025 provided the additional fee is also paid within this period. - R51(2) EPC; GL, A-X, 5.2.4 Renewal fees 
- In this case: 3 Apr 2025 + 6m = 3 Oct 2025 -> [Friday but EPO closed] -> R134(1) EPC -> 6 Oct 2025 (Mon). [J 1/81]
- Additional fee is 50 % of the belated renewal fee - A2(1).5 RFeesEPC

(c) What would be your answer if priority was claimed from an earlier application filed on 3 September 2021?
In that case:
- EP entry deadline: 3 Sep 2021 [A2(xi) PCT] + 31m [A22(3) PCT; R159(1) EPC; R131(4) EPC] = 3 Apr 2024.
- Third year renewal fee date (always calculated from DATE OF FILING): 3 Sep 2022 [A2(xi) PCT] + 2 yr = 3 Sep 2024 + end of month = 30 Sep 2024.  Falls LATER than EP entry date. So MUST NOT be paid immediately on EP entry. Calculated using standard R51(1) EPC date.
- So renewal fee [A2(1).4 RFeesEPC] can be paid without additional fee [A2(1).5 RFeesEPC] at latest on 30 Sep 2024.
- If not paid by then, may still be validly paid within 6m of 30 Sep 2024 - R51(2) EPC
- The additional fee can be paid until the last day of the sixth month following the month containing the anniversary of the date of filing (see J 4/91, reasons 2.7)
[de ultimo ad ultimo; J 4/91].
- In this case: 30 Sep 2024 + 6m [de ultimo ad ultimo; J 4/91] = 31 Mar 2025.

Question 2
L4-11 (EQE - 6 points)

US company A filed an international application PCT1 with the USPTO. 
The application was searched by the USPTO and published by the International Bureau. 
Company A wants examination of PCT1 in the European phase before the EPO to be started as soon as possible.
What steps have to be taken?

- dO may not process IA unless expressly allowed by the Applicant [A23(1), A23(2), A40(1), A40(2) PCT, OJ EPO 2013, p. 156].

Representation: 
- EPO requires representation for applicants who are not residents of EPC CS - A133(2) EPC
- EPO as dO of PCT1 applies EPC concerning who needs to be represented - A27(7); R51bis.1(b) PCT
- US company may carry out early EP entry themselves and pay required fees. Anything after that (including FP/RE or correction of deficiencies) needs to be done by EPO representative. [A133(2)EPC; Euro-PCT Guide 5.3.006; GL, E-IX, 2.3.1; GL, A-X, 1.]
- Agent used in int. phase may not be used before the EPO unless they are also EPO representative - GL, E-IX, 2.3.1
- A separate representative appointment for EP phase is needed even if int. phase agent is EPO representative - GL, E-IX, 2.3.1
- Best to appoint representative ASAP otherwise invitation will be sent out by EPO - R163(5) EPC

Minimal requirements for early entry into EP phase:
- search fee - R159(1)(e) EPC
- supply translation if necessary [R159(1)(a) EPC].  In this case, USPTO did the search and the application is published so it must have been filed in English. Hence, translation not necessary for EPO and PCT pub will become EP pub - A153(3) EPC
- specify application documents - R159(1)(b) EPC
- pay filing fee - R159(1)(c) EPC; A78(2) EPC, R38(1) EPC;
- pay designation fee if European search report is already published - R159(1)(d) EPC; R39(1); A79(2) EPC. Within 6m of search report pub. 
- in this case, EPO will need to carry out a supplementary European search [A153(7) EPC] but the ISR takes place of the ESR for PCT1 [A153(6) EPC].
- since examination happened in int. phase and IA is published, ISR is more than likely published with it, so it is highly likely that the designation fee may already be paid. in either case, it seems it will be possible to pay the designation fee before having to wait out the full 31m period. 
- pay the renewal fee in respect of the third year if the fee has fallen earlier - A86(1) EPC, R51(1) EPC
- file written request for examination within 6m of ISR publication date - R159(1)(f) EPC if the R70(1) EPC period already expired. Including payment of examination fee - A94(1) EPC; A2(1).6 RFeesEPC.
- Again, EPO will need to do its own search, but applicant can already file request for examination on entry to EP and waive the R70(2)/R70a(2) invitations.
- Waive right to receive R161, R162 EPC communications to accelerate.
- If ISR from USPTO was positive, PPH may be used to potentially accelerate grant at EPO - OJ 2014, A8

- in this case, EPO will need to carry out a supplementary European search [A153(7) EPC] but the ISR takes place of the ESR for PCT1 [A153(6) EPC].

in case (b)?

- 3 extra claim fees are incurred because claim count is over 15 on EP entry - R162(1), R162(2) EPC; A2(1).15 RFeesEPC. This can be paid on EP entry or within 6m from the R162 EPC communication.

[R69.2(i) PCT] or at 27m after prio under R69.2(ii) PCT. These are both AFTER the 26m from prio deadline for paying designation fee at the earlier date. 
- Therefore designation fee must be paid on EP entry at 31m from filing date. - Euro-PCT Guide 5.8.001; (and extension and validation fees, if applicable; Euro-PCT Guide 5.8.012) - R159(1)(d) EPC; R39(1), A79(2) EPC.
- Examination fee will be reduced by 75 % because EPO was IPEA - RFees 14(2); OJ 2018, A4.

Question 4
L4-18 (Selection) (EQE - 10.5 points)

Today, 11 March 2025, you receive a letter from a US client asking you to perform the necessary acts for entering the regional phase before the EPO for an international application, if still possible. He filed the international application on 16 July 2023 with the US Patent Office (USPTO). The priorities of US applications dated 16 July 2022 and 22 October 2022 were claimed.
International search and preliminary examination was carried out by the USPTO. The subject-matter of each of the original fifteen claims met the requirements of PCT Article 33(1) to (4).
Since the period for performing the necessary acts for entering the regional phase before the EPO has already lapsed, your client proposes to withdraw the first of the priority claims in order to extend this period.
(a) Will withdrawal of the first priority claim have the desired effect?
(b) If this priority claim were maintained, would it still be possible to enter the regional phase before the EPO and, if so, what measures should be taken?

(a)
- Entry to regional/national phase is calculated from date of earliest priority claimed when there are multiple priority claims - A2(xi)(b) PCT.
- So removing the oldest priority claim [16 JUL 2022] would, in principle, shift the date for re-calculating the EP entry period to just the single left-over priority claim of [22 OCT 2022] under A2(xi)(a) PCT.
- Withdrawing priority claim made under A8(1) PCT/A4C PC is possible under PCT at any time prior to expiration of 30 months from the priority date - R90bis.3(a) PCT
- If there are multiple priority claims, its possible to withdraw just one of them - R90bis.3(b) PCT
- However, only time limits that have NOT ALREADY EXPIRED will be re-computed from the new priority date - R90bis.3(d) PCT
- 16 JUL 2022 [A2(xi) PCT] + 31m [R159.1(a) EPC] = 16 FEB 2025 (Sun) -> [R134(1) EPC] -> 17 FEB 2025 (Mon)
- Today is 11 MAR 2025, so this limit has already expired and will not be recalculated - R90bis.3(d) PCT.
- Moreover, we are also past the 30m expiration under R90bis.3(a) PCT. So NO priority withdrawal may be carried out at all for the first priority claim this application under the PCT.
- Withdrawal of the first priority will not have the desired effect.

(b) 
- 31m limit for EP entry was 17 FEB 2025 (Mon)
- This was 22 days ago.
- Applicant can pay omitted fees, request further processing (FP) under A121 EPC within a period of 2m from R160(2) EPC notification and pay the FP fee for each of the omitted acts - R135(1) EPC, A2(1).12 RFeesEPC
- 22 days is less than 2m, so FP is available for EP entry.
- Alternatively, if all due care is proven, re-establishment of rights (RE) in the 2m period for FP is under R135(1) EPC is possible if all due care criterion is met. - A122 EPC; R136(3) EPC. 
- For each omitted act, RE fee has to be paid and the omitted act has to be completed, resulting in an RE and FP fee for each omitted act under R159(1) EPC. 
- So better to request FP under A121(1) EPC; R135(1) EPC.
- USPTO did the ISR. So IA had to be published in English. So no translation and FP fee for translation is needed under R159(1)(a) EPC.
- no FP fee is due for specifying the documents under R159(1)(b) [GL E-VIII 3.1.3]
- Filing fee (and any additional page fees GL, A-III, 13.2) under R159(1)(c) EPC and FP fee (comprising 50 % of filing fee + 50 % of additional fee) need to be paid.
- Designation fee and FP fee for that - R159(1)(d). It has to be paid on entry because we are 31m past priority date.
- Search fee will have to be paid because EPO needs to do its own search as USPTO was ISA in int. phase - A153(7) EPC; OJ 2009, 594. 1 FP fee for that as well - R159(1)(e) EPC
- Request for examinaton + examination fee (unitary act) = 1 FP fee comprising 50 % flat fee + 50 % examination fee) - R159(1)(f)
- No additional claims fees need to be paid as there are only 15 claims - R162(1) EPC
- Renewal fee is calculated from FILING date - NOT prio date - R51(1) EPC
- Renewal fee is due on 31 JUL 2025. 
- It can be paid 6m in advance - R51(1) EPC
- So can be paid today.
- After expiry of 31m period, only professional rep. can perform FP acts for US client - A27(7) PCT; Art. 133(2) EPC
- If US client doesnt appoint one, EPO will invite him to do so within 2m - R163(5) EPC

Question 7
L4-39 (EQE 2014) (6 points)

International application PCT-CN was filed by your client, a large Chinese company, on 29 October 2022 in Chinese with the Chinese patent office (CNIPA). The publication of PCT-CN contains 40 pages of description and 2 pages of claims. In order to enter the European phase, your client provides you with an English translation of the description of PCT-CN comprising 35 pages and an amended set of claims in English comprising 3 pages. These documents will be submitted to the EPO by post together with EPO Form 1200. 
a)  Your client contacts you today, 11 March 2025, and wants to know what the amount of the filing fee will be. 

- Filing fee [A78(2) EPC] for Euro-PCT entry depends on whether Form 1200 was filed online or not. In this case, it was filed by post so the flat filing fee will be 285 EUR - A2(1).1(ii) RFees EPC
- for a European patent application comprising more than 35 pages, the additional fee is 17 EUR for the 36th and each subsequent page - R38(2) EPC; A2(1).1a RFees EPC
- This is calculated on the basis of IA publication; in Chinese in this case - GL/EPO A-III 13.2
- If pages are amended, translation of IA as required under A153(4) EPC and R159(1)(a) EPC are taken as basis instead.  So translation of description and claims is taken as basis. - GL/EPO A-III 13.2; OJ EPO 2009, 338
- in this case, then we have translated description and AMENDED claims. So original Chinese description has not been amended and will form basis for page fee calculation with 40 pages. 
- claims have been amended. so translated version will form basis for page fee calculation with 3 pages.
- SO total is 43 pages + 1 page of bibliographic data/abstract = 44 pages. surplus pages are 44 - 35 = 9 pages. 
- 9 x 17 EUR = 153 EUR.
- Added to flat filing fee is 153 EUR + 285 EUR = 438 EUR.

b)  What is the latest date for paying the filing fee without incurring additional fees? 

- Filing fee including any additional page fees must be paid on EP entry. - R159(1)(c) EPC; A78(2) EPC, R38(1) EPC, A2(1).1 and .1a RFeesEPC
- Additional page fee paid as part of filing fee if EP app comprises more than 35 pages - GL/EPO A-III 13.2
- This is payable as part of the filing fee within 31m period of R159(1) EPC as part of the filing fee.
- Latest possible day for EP entry is 31m from filing (prio) date of PCT-CN. - A22(3) PCT
- 29 Oct 2022 [A2(xi) PCT] + 31m [R131(4) EPC] = 29 May 2025 [EPO closed] -> R134(1) EPC -> 30 May 2025 (Fri).
- the latest date for paying the filing fee without incurring additional fees is 30 May 2025.

Question 8
2017 Paper D1 - QUESTION 1 		(6 MARKS) 

On 7 August 2014, applicant A filed PCT-A with the EPO as receiving Office. PCT-A does not claim priority of an earlier application and contains two claims, independent claim 1 and independent claim 2. In February 2015, the applicant received from the EPO as International Searching Authority (ISA) a partial search report for the first invention and an invitation to pay an additional search fee for the second invention. The applicant did not respond to the invitation. The applicant received an international preliminary report on patentability (IPRP) repeating the lack of unity objection and with a positive opinion for the first invention. 
Today (7 March 2017), the applicant would like to have the second invention searched by the EPO. 
Consider the following two situations: 
a) Advise the applicant on how to have the first invention granted as a European patent as soon as possible and to get the second invention searched. 

- 7 Aug 2014 to 7 Mar 2017 is exactly 31m
- 7 Mar 2017 is a working day at the EPO. So it is the last day for entering EP phase without additional fees - R159(1) EPC; A22(3) PCT; R50 PCT

How to get EP granted ASAP:
- carry out all acts necessary to enter EP phase TODAY, i.e.,:
- no translation needed under R159(1)(a) EPC as EPO was ISA so PCT-A is already in DE or EN or FR;
- specify application documents - R159(1)(b) EPC
- pay filing fee - R159(1)(c) EPC; A78(2) EPC, R38(1) EPC;
- pay designation fee if European search report is already published - R159(1)(d) EPC; R39(1); A79(2) EPC. Within 6m of search report pub. 
- in this case, EPO will not need to carry out a supplementary European search under A153(7) EPC but the ISR will take place of the ESR for PCT1 [A153(6) EPC].
- pay the renewal fee in respect of the third year if the fee has fallen earlier - A86(1) EPC, R51(1) EPC
- file written request for examination  - R159(1)(f) EPC if the R70(1) EPC period already expired. Including payment of examination fee - A94(1) EPC; A2(1).6 RFeesEPC.
- Again, EPO will need to do its own search, but applicant can already file request for examination on entry to EP and waive the R70(2)/R70a(2) invitations.
- Waive right to receive R161, R162 EPC communications to accelerate - OJ 2015 A93
- search fee FOR THE FIRST INVENTION under R159(1)(e) EPC need not be paid because EPO was ISA and already did the search. - A153(7) EPC, R164(2) EPC; OJ 2022 A29
- amend the claim set on EP entry to remove independent claim 2 to be in keeping with A82 EPC. indicate documents under R159(1)(b) EPC to be the new amended claim set containing only independent claim 1. remove any conflicting parts of the description to avoid any office actions under A94(3) EPC asking to correct description, etc. 
- Respond to ISR drafted by EPO when filing the amended claim set indicating that you are proceeding with the application. 

How to get second invention searched:

- entering EP phase with both claims and making arguments as to their unity will not work because applicant did not respond to partial ISR in int. phase.  Will need to restrict to one invention only; only first invention in claim set was searched - G 2/92
- file divisional [A76(1) EPC; R36 EPC] while parent is still pending and make independent claim 2 the first claim of the divisional. keep the exact same description as for the parent (at least for now);
- pay search fee and filing fee within 1m of filing the divisional - R36(3) EPC 
Alternatively:
- enter EP phase with both claims in the claim set and pay additional search fee to have EPO search independent claim 2 after EP entry. once supplementary ESR is drawn up for the second invention, decide whether it's worth pursuing and file a divisional while the parent is still pending [A76(1) EPC; R36 EPC] for the second invention. 



b) The applicant only wants to pursue one European patent application but does not know yet for which invention he wishes to obtain protection. Advise the applicant on which steps to take to be able to base this decision on a search from the EPO for both inventions. 

- EPO was ISA so the first invention had already been searched by the EPO. 
- To get the second invention searched, he can either file a divisional once parent enters EP and pay the required fees, including a new search fee; 
- or he can enter EP phase with all (and maybe amended) claims, indicating the claim to be searched to be independent claim 2. 
- EPO will issue invite under R164(2)(a) EPC to pay a search fee within 2m.
- If he decides to pursue invention 1 or 2, he will have to restrict the application to a single invention - G 2/92; C-III, 3.2.2; H-II, 6.4.2 - R164(2)(b)(c) EPC.
