Question 1
H3-07	(10.5 points)


Will a date of filing be accorded to the European patent application in the following situations? If so, what will be the filing date? If applicable, what further requirements have to be met?
In each case the documents are filed directly with the EPO in Munich on 3 March 2019 and the requirements of Rule 40(1)(a) and (b) are fulfilled.

General considerations:
- EPO office in Munich is competent filing office for EP applications - A75(1); R35(1)
- All three requirements (a) to (c) of R40(1) need to be satisfied to accord a filing date. - R40(1)
- Nationality of applicant does not matter for according filing date - A58
- Language of application does not matter for according filing date - A14(2)
- No representation required for act of filing - A133(1) and A133(2)
- "Filing date" = "date of filing" = "DoF"

(a) Upon filing the application the Dutch applicant makes reference to a Dutch national application filed on 3 January 2018.
Upon invitation from the EPO, a certified copy and translation into English of the earlier filed application and a set of claims are filed on 12 May 2019.

- DoF accorded as R40(1)(c) requirement met by referring to Dutch app - R40(1)(c)
- Need to indicate filing date of Dutch app; its file number; the office of filing; and indicate that this reference replaces the description and any drawings - R40(2)
- It does not matter that the Dutch app was filed more than 12m from the EP app DoF. No requirement to claim prio to the Dutch app - GL A-II, 4.1.3.1
- The applicant must supply a copy of the previously filed application certified as correct by the authority with which it was filed within two months of filing the application - R40(3)
- 3 MAR 2019 + 2m [R131(4)] -> 3 MAY 2019 [EPO open]
- The copy was not filed by then and EPO requested certified copy with R55 communication with a non-extendable 2m period - GL A-II, 4.1.5
- 12 May 2019 is comfortably under 2m from earliest possible R55 notification date of 3 May 2019.
- DoF filing is retained if certified copy provided within 2m period of R55 - A90(2); GL A-II, 4.1.5; R55
-  Translation of Dutch app is necessary under R40(3) for examination but not for according a DoF - GL, A-II, 4.1.4
- Set of claims irrelevant for getting a filing date - R40(1)
- DoF accorded as 3 MAR 2019.

(b) Upon filing the application the French applicant Mr. F makes reference to a French national application filed on 7 March 2018. At the same time, the applicant files a set of claims. Mr. F is neither the applicant nor the successor in title of the French national application.

- Set of claims irrelevant for getting a filing date - R40(1)
- DoF accorded as R40(1)(c) requirement met by referring to FR app - R40(1)(c)
- Need to indicate filing date of FR app; its file number; the office of filing; and indicate that this reference replaces the description and any drawings - R40(2)
- Applicant/successor in title status irrelevant between EP app and app to which reference is made under R40(1)(c); R40(2) - GL A-II, 4.1.3.1 - this would only matter if EP app claimed prio to the FR app, but here it does not.
- DoF accorded as 3 MAR 2019.

(c) Upon filing the application the French applicant makes reference to an earlier filed French national application indicating that the reference also replaces the claims.
In the Request-for-Grant form, the applicant has omitted to indicate the file number of the previously filed application.
Upon invitation from the EPO, the missing file number is filed on 4 April 2019. At the same time a certified copy of the earlier filed application is filed.

- omission of file number is a deficiency under R40(2)
- triggers R55 invite with 2m non-extendable period to correct deficiency
- 4 APR 2019 is well within 2m of any possible invitation date considering app was filed on 3 MAR 2019
- 2m period for filing certified copy is satisfied - R40(3)
- However, DoF accorded will be 4 Apr 2019 because conditions of R40(2) were not fulfilled on filing.

(d) Upon filing the application the Chinese applicant makes reference to an earlier filed Chinese application.
Upon invitation from the EPO, a certified copy of the earlier Chinese application is not filed.

- not providing the certified copy after R55 invite results in application not being treated as an EP application - A90(2)
- no DoF accorded in that case.
- No FP and no RE available because there is no EP app to speak of - A121; A122


(e) Upon filing the application the Taiwanese applicant makes reference to a Taiwanese application filed on 6 March 2018. In the Request-for-Grant form, the applicant indicates that he claims priority from the earlier filed application. A certified copy of the earlier Taiwanese application is included in the filing.
However, the applicant does not file a translation of the earlier Taiwanese application.

- Requirements of R40(1) are all met.
- Priority likely validly claimed as:
-- within 12m under A87(1)
-- from a WTO member state under A87(1)
- certified copy included under - R40(3)
- translation must be filed within 2m for progressing examination. If not, R58 invite sent out to cure deficiency.
- However, this is irrelevant for according a filing date. - GL, A-II, 4.1.4
- DoF accorded as 3 MAR 2019.

Question 3
H3-19 (Selection) 	(EQE - 5 points) 

In January 2018, Applicant A and applicant B filed a European patent application EP-P, which is still pending. Applicant A designated NL, and applicant B designated BE. All other designations were withdrawn on filing.
 A divisional application EP-D, filed recently in the name of applicant A, is divided out from application EP-P. 
Applicant A asks you if EP-D will be accepted as a divisional application. What do you advise? 

- NO, it will not be accepted as a divisional application.
- EP-P is pending. Hence, a divisional app may be filed - A76(1); R36(1); G 1/09
- Multiple applicants designating different EP CSs allowed for EP apps - A59
- preserves unity of application under A118
- Withdrawing designation possible at any time before grant - A79(3)
- Divisional must be filed in name of all applicants - J 2/01
- EP-D only filed in name of A. This makes it not validly filed and it will not be dealt with as an EP app - A90(2). 
- Solution is to re-file together in name of A and B and transfer rights from B to A under A72.
- All designations not withdrawn from the parent need to be designated for the EP-D - A76(2); G 4/98
- So if A is interested in designating NL only for EP-D, he needs to withdraw BE designation after transfer of rights - A79(3).

Question 4
H3-22 	(10.5 points) 

Last week, on 19 February 2019, a Korean applicant living in Korea submits an EPO form 1001, together with the name and address of his representative. Section 26 of the form refers to an earlier Korean patent application "KR14-May-2017 2017014231", and a request is made to replace the description and drawings by this reference. No other indications are made in section 26, and no other documents are subsequently filed. 
(a)  Will a date of filing be initially accorded to the application? 

(b)  What must the applicant then do for the application to retain the filing date? 

(c)  What further steps are needed in relation to the Korean description?

(a) 
- Anyone can file EP application regardless of nationality - A58
- Applicants resident outside EPC CSs need professional representation for acts other than filing of EP app - A133(2)
- Can be filed in any language provided translation into FR/DE/EN is provided - A14(2)
- Form 1001 sufficient to satisfy R40(1)(a); GL A-III 4.1
- Name and address of representative enough to satisfy R40(1)(b) - A-II, 4.1.2
- Reference to previously filed app is needed under R40(1)(c).
- To satisfy R40(1)(c), ALL aspects of R40(2) must be fulfilled. 
- In this case, we have indication of replacing drawings (iv); presumed filing date (i), office (iii) and presumed app number (ii).
- So it should be sufficient to get a DoF under A80.
- If not, can always put down those date and number of the KR app and correct under R139 once clarified with the applicant what the actual date is.
- Or could always file 30 EP applications today in the applicant's name, putting down another day of May 2017 and only pay filing fee for one of them once clarified with applicant what the actual date is.

(b) To retain filing date, the Applicant will then need to:
- supply certified copy of the KR app under R40(3) within 2m of filing the app: 19 FEB 2019 + 2m [R131(4)] -> 19 APR 2019 [EPO closed] -> R134(1) -> 23 APR 2019. Still time to do this.
- EPO has no access to the KR app itself - OJ 2009, 486; GL A-II 4.1.3.1
- If we miss it, we get R55 invitation anyway and a further 2m from date of communication - GL A-II 4.1.5
- This will retain the filing date.
- Otherwise not treated as EP app - A90(2), R55

(c)
- Desc needs to be translated into FR/DE/EN within 2m of DoF - A14(2); R6(1)
- That's 23 APR 2019. Still time to do this.
- If not done on time, EPO will send A90(3), (4); R57(a); R58 invitation with further 2m to do it.
- Claim will need to be provided eventually - R57(c), which will need to be directly and unambiguously derivable from the description as they were not incorporated by reference on the date of filing from the KR app.

Question 7
2017 Paper D1 - QUESTION 3 	(4 MARKS) 

Today, 7 March 2017, you receive an e-mail from a patent attorney in Japan. He asks you to file a European patent application claiming the priority of Japanese patent application JP 12345, containing a description, claims and drawings, filed in Japanese at the Japan Patent Office (JPO) on 7 March 2016. The text of the e-mail does not contain any details about the applicant but mentions two attachments: one attachment with a copy of JP 12345 as filed, and one attachment with the applicant’s name and contact details. However, the attachments were corrupted and cannot be opened. 
In the e-mail the patent attorney in Japan informed you that he cannot be contacted today. However, you were instructed to file the application today. 
How will you proceed? 

Securing DoF:
- Today is last day of 12m prio period under A87(1)
- Cannot ask for extension of this 12m period because it is not a period to be specified under R132(2) EPC
- However, priority claim may be added/corrected under R52(2) and R52(3) EPC, respectively.
- We should secure a DoF [A80] of 7 MAR 2017 by meeting all the three requirements of R40(1).
- We need an indication that EP patent is sought - R40(1)(a) - file a cover letter when submitting files online today to the EPO stating that an EP application is filed herewith
- We need to incorporate JP 12345 by reference under R40(1)(c) - just file a statement saying at least description and drawings are incorporated by reference. We have the office where it was filed [Japan PTO] and file number [12345] and filing date [7 MAR 2016] so we will meet all the requirements of R40(2) and the DoF of the EP app will not be re-dated.
- We need to file a certified copy of JP 12345 within 2m [R40(3)] and a translation into one of the official EPO languages [A14(2)] to retain the DoF through incorporation by reference under R40(1)(c).
- Don't need claims for now to secure DoF - could always just submit them within 2m of DoF after figuring out whether they are useful for EP procedure and do not violate A123(2). 
- Can also just say claims are incorporated on filing to satisfy R57(c) immediately by using Section 26 of Request for Grant form - GL A-II, 4.1.3.1
- The name and address of the applicant's representative is sufficient as information to contact applicant under R40(1)(b) - GL A-II, 4.1.2
- Since Applicant is outside of EPC CS (Japan), he needs representation before the EPO anyway - A133(2)
- I am his representative and EPO has my contact details as I am on the list mentioned in A134(5)
- So filing date will be accorded for meeting all the requirements of R40(1).

Adding priority claim:
- We have 16m from earliest prio date to file a declaration claiming priority to JP 12345 - A88(1); R52(2)
- 7 MAR 2016 + 16m [R131(4)] -> 7 JUL 2017 [EPO open]
- We still have 4m to file the R52(2) declaration, so it should be plenty of time to find out the exact details of JP 12345, add the priority claim under R52(2) and provide the certified copy and translation [R40(3)].
