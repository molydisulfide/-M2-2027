Question 1
2015 Paper D1 - QUESTION 1 	(9 MARKS) 


On 17 February 2015, applicant A filed on the last day of the priority period European patent applications EP1 and EP2, together with copies of the earlier national British patent applications UK1 and UK2. UK1 and UK2 relate to different inventions. 
EP1 claims the priority of the earlier national patent application UK1. The description and claims of EP1 and UK1 are identical. 
EP2 claims the priority of the earlier national patent application UK2. The description and claims of EP2 and UK2 are identical. 
On 20 February 2015, the contents of UK1 and UK2 were made publicly available. 
Later the applicant noticed that he had forgotten to file the drawings for EP1. On 23 February 2015 he filed drawings for EP1 and he also filed drawings for EP2. The drawings for EP1 are identical to the drawings of UK1. The drawings for EP2 are newly drafted, because UK2 does not contain any drawings. 
Today, on 24 Feb 2015, the applicant asks you what can be done to optimise the situation with regard to EP1 and EP2. 

Timeline:
- UK1 and UK2 filed @ 17 FEB 2014 [different inventions]
- EP1 and EP2 filed @ 17 FEB 2015. Claims/spec identical to UK1 and UK2, respectively. Prio claimed respectively. Drawings missing for both.
- UK1 and UK2 published @ 20 FEB 2015.
- EP1 and EP2 drawings filed @ 23 FEB 2015. EP1 drawings = UK1 drawings. EP2 drawings = new matter over UK2.
- TODAY @ 24 FEB 2015. We need to "optimise" situation.

Consider EP1 first:
- Prio validly claimed within 12m period - A87(1)
- Effective date is 17 FEB 2014 - A89
- Publication of UK1 will spoil novelty of added drawings of EP1 if EP1 filing date is redated to 23 FEB 2015 - A54(2)
- Cannot refile EP1 claiming prio to UK1 and including drawings because 12m period is already gone - A87(1).
- If ALL drawings are missing, this qualifies as missing parts under R56.
- Redating can be avoided under R56(3) if the missing parts are completely contained in the priority document. This is the case here.
- In order to avail of remedy of R56(3): (a) a copy of UK1 has to be provided to the EPO within 2m of the filing date accorded under R40(1); (b) an indication as to where the missing drawings are completely contained needs to be provided to the EPO within 2m of DoF - R56(1); R56(2); R56(3).
- 17 FEB 2015 + 2m [R134(1)] -> 17 APR 2015 [EPO open]. Still time to act today and provide the copy of UK1 and indicate where the missing drawings are contained. This effective incorporation by reference will save the filing date of EP1 and hence the effective date under A89, while ensuring the drawings are parts of the original disclosure of EP1. 
- Simultaneous request along with the R56(3) requirements may be filed within a single submission - GL A-II 5.4.1
- The "completely contained" requirement applicable to late-filed missing drawings also requires them to be in the same format as the drawings of the priority application, meaning that the drawings filed under Rule 56(3) may contain colour or greyscale content only to the same extent as the drawings in the priority application; see the notice from the EPO dated 5 September 2025 (OJ EPO 2025, A57).
- The copy of the priority application that is required for the request according to Rule 56(3) does not need to be certified. - GL A-II 5.4.3

Consider EP2 now:
- Here, the situation is materially different as there were no drawings at all in UK2. So we cannot user R56 to add missing parts and use our priority claim to incorporate them by reference.
- The missing drawings in this case have essentially been provided under the provisions of R56(2). 
- This will result in redating of EP2 to 23 FEB 2015, losing priority claim due to not meeting 12m period of A87(1) and publication of UK2 becoming full A54(2) prior art against EP2.

How to preserve priority date of EP2:
- We have right to withdraw the filed missing elements under R56(6) within 1m of the notification from the EPO that EP2 has been redated - R56(2). In that case, re-dating shall be deemed to not have been made.
- Earliest possible deadline in that regard would be 23 MAR 2015 [EPO open]. Today there is still time to act and withdraw the drawings from EP2 under R56(6). This will keep our priority date and not make UK2 prior art against EP2 - A89; A54(2).

Considerations of somehow including the new drawings into the disclosure of EP2:
- UK2 and UK1 are different inventions. UK2 has no drawings at all. EP2 drawings are effectively new matter with earliest possible effective date being the DoF of EP2. EP2 only claimed prio to UK2 on its DoF. 
- If we are sure that drawings of EP2 will be novel and inventive over the disclosures of UK2, we can go ahead and keep them in EP2 and keep the re-dated DoF. But claims and desc of EP2 will be not novel over UK2 publication. Will need to rewrite claims within A123(2) but can be based on the drawings if directly and unambiguously derivable. 
- Probably not worth it.
- Cannot file EP3 claiming partial prio to EP2 and adding drawings because EP2 was not the first app for the invention already disclosed in UK2.

Question 2
H4-08		(EQE - 4.5 points)

The Search Division informs the applicant in accordance with Rule 64(1) EPC that the application contains three inventions, and that with respect to the second and third invention a further search fee has to be paid for each of them if the European search report is to cover these inventions. A further search fee is only paid for the second invention. In the first communication from the Examining Division, the objection of non-unity is maintained in respect of the three inventions.
What must be done to obtain the desired protection in the following cases?
(a) The applicant decides that he only wishes to continue with invention two as the only practical embodiment and wishes to abandon the claims for inventions one and three.
(b) The applicant wishes to obtain protection for all three inventions, with minimum cost.

(a) 
- Since the search fee (Rfees2(1).2) has validly been paid for invention two, the Applicant may proceed towards grant of invention two based on this application. 
- Matter pertaining to inventions one and three may simply be excised from the application to reach unity of invention under A82; R44
- Prosecution may continue based on matter directed to invention two only, which had been searched and can thus be validly pursued and used to amend the claims in view of cited prior art from the search report (G 2/92; R137(5)) 
- Search fee for invention one will not be refunded in that case - 

(b) 
- Two search fees have already been paid for I1 and I2. These will not be refunded unless a R64(2) refund is requested and the non-unity finding is found to be unjustified. 
- Either way; a divisional application will have to be filed to get a patent for matter related to I1 - A76(1); R36(1)
- This has to be done while this app is still pending - G 1/09; GL A-IV 1.1.1.
- Same for I3.
- The cost will be effectively 3 search fees + 3 exam fees, etc. anyway. - R38(1)
- But the search fee paid under R38(1) for the divisionals will be refunded under Rfees 9(2) - in this case in full because the claims/matter will be indentical to that already searched by the EPO - OJ 2024, A6
- Appeal fee is more than search fee. Not worth requesting a non-unity decision and appealing it if we are looking to minimize costs.
- Either way, a single application can only lead to grant of a patent for a single invention. So no matter if there is or isnt unity, a divisional MUST be filed, unless the applicant is happy to somehow have protection for I2 as a dependent claim from I1 and this counts to him as "protection".
- to minimize costs further; ensure page fees under R38(2) and claims fees R45 are kept to minimum (ideally zero).
- file online to get a lower online filing fee - RFees2(1).1
